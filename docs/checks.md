# Checks

The checks the validator runs on the records of `docs/data-model.md`. Section and field names refer to that file; the profile format is in `docs/profiles.md`.

The checks are in `src/smd_engine/validate/`, one module per subject, run in the order of its `REGISTRY`; hints run only when the record's profile enables them. `smd validate --hints` prints after each hint the procedure and section that act on it ("→ procedures/functional-plan.md §4"; the table is `HINT_PROCEDURES` in `validate/context.py`, and a hint of its own half or case may name another). An unknown profile, or a board, kind or relation kind outside the profile, is an error reported with the schema errors, and like them it stops the checks:
- **Errors:** duplicate ids; `fillers` on an element that is not a role; a `detail` row whose length differs from `columns`; dangling references in `seen_from`, `in`, relations, transitions, qualification targets, move sources and thesis `feeds`; `seen_from` that is neither a `position` nor a `reflexive_position` (a `place_for_others` is refused); `reflected_from` that is neither a `position` nor a `reflexive_position` (gate Г5а Б; a `place_for_others` is refused); `half` rules; containment cycles; transition numbering; a closed K without `closed_in`; a K raised after the record's cycle; a record revising itself; `verified` without a checker; unknown source ids when a manifest is given.
- **Errors (ingestion):** `of` on an element that is not a gap; `performed_by` on a non-function or not naming a position or place for others; a thesis that `raises` an unknown K; a hypothesis result without its text, field artefact or `resolved_in`; dangling hypothesis targets or K; a thesis status that does not fit its voice; an accepted move from an AI voice with no `accept_move` sign-off; a sign-off by an AI voice, without a target, or aimed at the wrong kind of target.
- **Warnings:** a screen label (`short`, or `label` when there is no `short`) over 60 characters, which the screen cuts; a kind off its usual board; a content item without a position; a transition inside one board; a `shift` between non-positions; a manifest entry with no file or url, or no clean-room decision.
- **Warnings (ingestion):** a revising cycle with no `reflected_from`; a thesis with no source address (except AI voice); an accepted move with neither a source nor a sign-off; a closed K, a `refined` thesis, a hypothesis result or a `verified` record with no matching sign-off; a hypothesis `testing` with no action; a sign-off that changes nothing, because its target is not in the state it licenses (only the latest K or move sign-off on a target is checked: `reopen_k` supersedes an earlier `close_k`).
- **Hints** (Girenko's criteria for a group screen, 17.10.2026): no entry; an empty board, or an empty half of a halved board (the program's small or big situation not laid out); one position and no places for others; a new frame without functions or with functions no one performs; open gaps; open qualifications; candidate moves, on elements and theses; AI variants awaiting the group.
- **Warnings (step 3):** subject-object talk ("we must", "our task", "мы должны", "наша задача") in a label on a board the profile flags `object` (the object board); `represents` not starting from a position; an ontology subject for a position outside `valid_for`.
- **Errors (step 3):** ontology `valid_for` that is not a position, `cooperation` that is not a cooperation element, `test` that is not a hypothesis, an id in `lives_in`, `covers` or `tempos` that is not an element.
- **Hints (classic method):** an ontology with no realizability test, or valid for several positions with no cooperation; an ontology whose `horizon` is longer than the `pace` of an element in `lives_in` or `covers`, or than a `tempos` entry: the continuum is exhausted before the horizon (`ontology-outlives-pace`, Khaprov, podcast 28.10.2026, l.154-172; no hint without `horizon`); a gap or a problem thesis with no scheme block; a new cycle with no `difficulty`; a hypothesis with no `revise_if`; degenerate belts (GP1987): mostly М-К theses and none in М ("play with words"), or М theses with none in мД (thinking cut off from the situation), or only мД theses, no М and no М-К ("mechanical reproduction").
- **Errors (G.P.'s «Программа действий»):** `stance` or `acts_as` on an element that is not a position, `goal_source`, `arises_from` or `assigned_by` on one that is not a goal, `nature` off an object board; an `arises_from` id that is neither a gap, a fact, nor a `difficulty`, `problem` or factual thesis; `assigned_by` that is not a position or place for others.
- **Warnings (G.P.'s «Программа действий»):** `reflected_from` naming a position or reflexive position with `stance: inside`.
- **Hints (G.P.'s «Программа действий», GP-Guide11, read against GP-T5):** an object-board element seen from a position with `stance: inside` (`object-from-inside`, ¶36); a new cycle whose object board is seen from an `external` position with no `reflected_from` (`external-without-reflection`, ¶35, ¶47); a goal with no breakdown: no `goal_source`, `arises_from`, gap linked by a relation, `difficulty` or `problem` thesis naming it, or `state.difficulty` (`goal-without-breakdown`, ¶43; also an `assigned` goal with no `assigned_by` and a `breakdown` goal with no `arises_from`); a position with `acts_as` (not `functionary`) and no `represents` relation (`position-without-object`, GP-Guide10 ¶7, ¶19); an object-board element with `modality: ought` and no fact linked by a relation, `in`, or a factual thesis (`ought-without-fact`, GP-T5 p. 252); `ought` on a `natural` element (`design-the-natural`, GP-STS); theses, other than `open`, with neither `feeds` nor `about` (`information-without-cell`, ¶5). Where the 1981 and 1988 texts disagree the 1988 lectures win: GP-Guide5 ¶7 required a forecast ("Управлять без прогноза в принципе невозможно"); GP-T5 p. 251 calls forecasting in оргуправление a substitution by scientistic principles, so `ought-without-fact` asks for the natural processes, not a forecast.
- **Hints (G.P.'s management scheme, `management-four-boards`, GP-T5):** a `leads` relation to someone with goals `goal_source: own` (seen from the target or from a position that `belongs` to it) (`lead-vs-manage`, pp. 169-170); a `manages` relation with no element the source `produces` and the target `uses` (`manage-via-organizedness`, pp. 222-223); a position that `manages` with no retrospective or no projective knowledge seen from it or linked to it (`manage-without-knowledge`, p. 241); a `leads` relation whose ends hold no post (itself, or one it `belongs` to), or with no line of `subordinates` from the leader's post down to the other (`leads-outside-organisation`, pp. 242, 254); a `represents` target outside the kinds expected for the position's `acts_as`: organiser to `post`, `functional_place`, `mission`, `monosystem`; leader to `post` (not `group`: the informal group is outside the administrative structure, pp. 224-225, 254); manager to `work_process`, `trajectory`, `self_motion`; politician to `club`, `position`; functionary to none (`representation-fit`; the table is the engine's reading of GP-T5 pp. 170-175, 213, 223, 242-244, 253-254, not a table of G.P.'s); an upper board with content but no past of the object (a `factual` kind, `knowledge_retro`, or `modality: fact` on the managed board), no `ideal_state`, or no `captures` from the upper board to the managed one (`development-step`, «шаг развития», «минимальная единица», pp. 229, 232, 238); a managed board with fewer than two monosystems, or monosystems with no `parasitic`, `symbiotic` or `mechanism_of` relation (`polysystem`, pp. 206-213).
- **Errors (Beer layer of `management-four-boards`, gate Бир3 В):** `vsm_system` on a kind the profile does not flag `vsm`; `of_whole` or `written` on a kind the profile does not flag `of_whole` (gates Г2 В, Г5б Б); `threshold` or `recovery_time` on an element that is neither an `algedonic_channel` nor marked `red_line: true`. The schema refuses a `vsm_system` that is not a non-empty list of distinct integers 1-5, an `of_whole` or `written` that is not a boolean, an empty `threshold` and a `recovery_time` that is not a number > 0. A profile that enables `single-node-metasystem`, `absorbed-into-system-1` or `no-model-of-whole` with no kind flagged `vsm`, or `no-model-of-whole` or `model-of-whole-one-filler` with no kind flagged `of_whole`, is refused.
- **Hints (Beer layer of `management-four-boards`, BEER1972):** one filler fills systems 3, 4 and 5 across the posts and positions it fills (`single-node-metasystem`, Гл. 9, 13): the transfer will remove the metasystem with the person. The filler of a post or position is the one actor in its `fillers`; a post with no fillers is filled by the one position that `belongs` to it; a position with no fillers that belongs to posts with one and the same filler is that filler; an element with several fillers, or a post with several positions, is shared (a multinode, Гл. 14) and counts for nobody. A red line (an element with `red_line: true`, or an `algedonic_channel` with a `threshold` or a `line`, gate КЛ1 В) and no recovery time (neither `recovery_time` nor `line.recovery_days`), or with no address: an `escalates` relation from it to a `post`, `position` or `place_for_others` (on `management-four-boards`, the only profile with `escalates`, only this counts), or, on a profile without `escalates`, a `flow` with `carries: information` to one (`line-without-recovery`, Гл. 17; all three profiles, robustness rule R6). A place filling system 5 that `captures` places of the business filling systems 4 and 5 between them (`absorbed-into-system-1`, Гл. 15): the business becomes part of the buyer's system 1, its name and identity at risk. The engine has no scenario table: a transfer scenario is laid out on the boards as the buyer's post and its `captures` relations (G.P.'s «захват», GP-T5 pp. 226-227). A model of the whole is a `model` with `of_whole: true`; a post or position holds it when it `uses` it (gate Г2 В). A post or position filling system 4 that holds no model of the whole, itself, through a position that `belongs` to it, through the post it belongs to or through another position of that post (`no-model-of-whole`, Гл. 13); the message says whether no model of the whole is on the boards or none is held there. A written model (`written: true`) does not count unless a place `uses` it: a written model still needs a keeper (gate Г7г А). A model of the whole whose holders all resolve to one named filler, by the rule above (`model-of-whole-one-filler`, Гл. 13): it leaves with the person at transfer. A model with `written: true` is written down and kept apart from the people and gets no such hint (gate Г5б Б): the person leaves, the record stays. A reflexive position counts by its fillers as a post does; a holder of another kind (a group, a place for others), a shared holder, or a holder with no named filler gives no hint, nor does a model nobody `uses`.
- **Errors (method review 31.10.2026, gates Ф В and У В; `validate/graph.py`):** a cycle of `belongs` relations, of `subordinates` relations, or of move sources (`move.from` of elements and theses, longer than the move from its own result reported above); each cycle is one error naming its ids. A cycle of `flow` is not an error: a position pays into a chain and receives its result.
- **Warnings (method review 31.10.2026):** an ontology position of `valid_for` with no line in `subjects` (positional-construction.md §5; the reverse of the warning of step 3).
- **Hints (functional plan, functional-plan.md §4; `validate/graph.py`):** over the functions `in` a new frame: a function with no payer (no `flow` into it from a position or place for others), no input (no `flow` into it) or no receiver (no `flow` from it to a position or place for others), each slot met as well by a gap linked to the function by a relation, with `of: position` for the payer, `material` or `means` for the input, `position`, `product` or `function` for the receiver (`function-flows`); functions of a frame with two or more linked by no `flow` (only `flow`) to another of them (`unlinked-functions`); positions, other than the cycle's reflexive one (`reflected_from`, `stance: reflexive`), that perform, pay for and receive nothing in the plan (`idle-positions`; places for others are left out). Enabled on `program-three-boards` and `classic-two-boards`; the management profile draws its functions on process boards, not in frames. With `carries` on a `flow` (`money`, `material`, `product`, `information`), the payer is an incoming flow of `money` from a position or place, an input an incoming flow of anything else, and a receiver an outgoing flow of anything but money to a position or place, or to another function of the same frame that has a receiver (followed along the chain, a cycle of functions walked once), so a middle function needs no line of its own to the receiver and only the end of the chain must reach a position or place; a supplier's `material` is an input and not a payer. A flow with no `carries` is read as before (any flow from a position or place pays, any flow in is an input, any flow out to a position or place receives), so records that never set it report what they did.
- **Errors (AI on the boards, gate ИИ1 Б):** `ai` on an element that is neither a function nor a role (a kind the profile flags `role`), or on a gap whose `of` is not `position`; `red_line` on a kind the profile flags `light_off` ("'red_line' is not for notes"; facts and signals on the default profile). `threshold` is allowed on any element with `red_line: true`, as the red line's free-text value. The schema refuses an `ai` other than `assists` or `performs` and a `red_line` that is not a boolean.
- **Hint `ai-decision-unanswered`** (functional-plan.md §3; `validate/ai.py`; all three profiles): a function whose effective `ai` (its own, else that of the role in `performed_by`) is `performs`, that is linked to a red line, and for which no role without `ai: performs` answers. A red line is an element with `red_line: true`, or an `algedonic_channel` with a `threshold` or a `line` (the management profile's carrier of a stakeholder's red line). Linked (`validate/redlines.py`, `linked`): the function is a red line itself, it is `in` one or one is `in` it, the role in its `performed_by` is one, or a relation of any kind, either way, joins it to one. A role answers when it is of a kind the profile flags `role`, not a gap (a missing role answers for nothing), has no `ai: performs`, and is the function's `performed_by`, or `subordinates` the function (role to function), or receives its `escalates` or a `flow` with `carries: information` (function to role). `subordinates` and `escalates` exist on the management profile only; elsewhere `performed_by` and the information flow back remain. A function with its own `ai: performs` whose `performed_by` is a role without `ai: performs` is answered by that role and gets no hint: the role answers for what AI does in it. The case behind it is the MIPT case's H64: some decisions cannot be passed on, to a model or to a new manager, without people of a certain role, and the owner records each as a yes/no red line. With no `ai` on the record the hint is silent.
- **Hint `red-line-without-value`** (functional-plan.md §4; all three profiles): an element with `red_line: true` and neither a `line` nor a `threshold`. A red line is an absolute value, a threshold or yes/no that data check (the case owner 26.10); the hint asks to name it. It is a hint, not an error: the group may mark a line before it has the value.
- **Errors (red line values, gate КЛ1 В; `validate/redlines.py`, `docs/red-lines.md`):** `line` on an element that is neither `red_line: true` nor an `algedonic_channel`; a `line` with both a corridor (`min`, `max`) and `expect`, or with neither; `min` not below `max`; `until` not after `since` (or after the record's `date` when there is no `since`); `line.recovery_days` and the channel's `recovery_time` both set and different; `observed` on an element with no `line`; an observed value of the wrong type (a boolean for a yes/no line, a number for a corridor); with a manifest, an unknown `observed` source. The schema refuses a `line` without `until`, and an observation without `value`, `date` or `source`. The schema also refuses `expect` without `metric`. **Warnings:** with a manifest, an observation whose source is not of kind `field_artefact`; observations dated before the line takes force (`since`, else the record's `date`), which are not read; two observations with the same date ("one observation per date").
- **Hints (red line values):** `red-line-breach` (field-result.md §5): the breaches of a line by its observations, each with its dates, by the rule of `src/smd_engine/redline.py` (observations from the line's start to `until`, sorted by date; a run of outside values spans from its first date to the next inside value, or, still open, to the later of the last observation and the record's `date`, never past `until`; it is a breach when longer than `line.recovery_days`, else `recovery_time`; any run when neither is set). `red-line-expired` (field-result.md §5): observations dated after `until`, or a record dated after it ("the line ended on <until>: renew or drop"). `red-line-outlives-pace` (functional-plan.md §4): a line in force (`until` minus `since`, or minus the record's `date`) more years than the `pace` of an element it is linked to (as above, itself included), the analogue of `ontology-outlives-pace`: a line is in force for a term matched to the pace of its tier (the case owner 26.10; the MIPT case's H24).
- **Hints (typical errors of a group screen, qualification-pass.md §3.2; `program-three-boards`):** positions, other than the reflexive one, with no `shift` or `flow` to another position, place or cooperation (`positions-unlinked`, E2); object-board content, gaps and notes aside, linked by no relation, transition, `in`, `performed_by` or move, however indirectly, to an element of another board (`object-isolated`, E3; an ontology's `cooperation` counts as linked to its `valid_for` positions; `seen_from` does not count, since E3 asks for a link besides it); facts with no relation, transition, `in`, `feeds` or `about` (`isolated-facts`, E6, E11); the starting position of a `shift`, other than the reflexive one, with nothing seen from it on a board with halves while a new frame is drawn (`jump-to-frame`, E12; the engine's reading: the order of the transitions is not read); a transition with no end that is a position, a place for others or seen from a position (`transition-unanchored`, E14). E1, E7 and the rest of E14 are the hints on halves, entry and `seen_from` above; the other rows need reading.
- **Hints (self-checks of the procedures):** an AI-drafted `cooperation` that no K targets (`ai-cooperation`, positional-construction.md §5); an ontology whose `valid_for` positions have no `flow` to or from its `cooperation` (`cooperation-flows`); a `detail` table with a column «что теряется» or "what is lost" whose cell is empty or says none (`нет`, `none`, `-`, `—`, `n/a`) in a row (`translation-loss`, stakeholder-ontology.md §5); on `program-three-boards`, a `garland` with fewer than two `belongs` relations into it, or whose events all cite one source ("one witness") (`garland`, visionary-tract.md §3); a bridge, a `situation` on the big half with a `respecify` move, whose move is not from a `model` and a `garland` (`bridge-from`, §5); a candidate move of the tract (`glue` on a model, `respecify` or `rescale` on a situation, `adjoin` on a goal) with no `via`; a move the group made in its talk needs none (`move-via`, §1). Not checked: that a stakeholder ontology names exactly one position (no field marks a record as one; several positions with no cooperation get the `ontology` hint), "one witness" by actor (signals carry no actor field), and three cycles in a row without a new scheme (the validator compares a record with its previous one only).
- **Errors (hypothesis standard, gates М1 В and Ф В; `validate/hypotheses.py`):** `observed` with k greater than n; the schema refuses an unknown `type`, a `rule` without `metric`, `op` or `threshold` or with another `op`, an `n_min` below 1, and an `observed` without `k`, `n` or `artefact`. `observed.artefact` is checked against the manifest like `artefacts`. The schema refuses `==` as an `op`: equality is no refutation condition.
- **Warnings (hypothesis standard):** a recorded `confirmed` while the whole Wilson interval of `observed` lies on the refuting side of the rule's threshold, or `refuted` while it lies on the holding side; with a manifest, an `observed.artefact` whose source is not of kind `field_artefact`.
- **Hints (hypothesis standard, `procedures/field-result.md` §0):** an untyped hypothesis whose `raised_in` cycle has typed hypotheses (`hypothesis-type`; records with none typed, like the MIPT case's, get none); a `claim` or `estimate` with neither `rule` nor `revise_if` (`hypothesis-rule`; such a hypothesis gets no `revise-if` hint, nor does a `core`, `design` or `probe`, and a `rule` stands in for `revise_if`); a `rule` with no `basis` (`rule-basis`); an `estimate` whose `rule` has no `n_min` (`rule-n-min`); a `probe` confirmed or refuted, or a `core` or `design` not `untested` (`type-status`); for each `testing` hypothesis with `observed`, the verdict its rule proposes (an `untested` one with `observed` gets the hint "observed data recorded but status is untested: set testing" instead), by the 95% Wilson score interval of k/n when the threshold is a share strictly between 0 and 1 (`verdict`, field-result.md §3: too few, refuted, confirmed, or inconclusive and stays testing; the group signs `accept_result`).
- **Hint `board-dense`:** a board, record boards included, with more elements than the profile's `board_dense` (default 15; `docs/profiles.md`), worded as advice to fold what is settled or split a part off, never an error; it points here, since no procedure folds a board. The default is low on purpose and tunable: working memory holds about four chunks when rehearsal and chunking are blocked (Cowan, N. (2001). The magical number 4 in short-term memory: a reconsideration of mental storage capacity. *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, 24(1), 87-114). That figure is about what one keeps in mind at once, not about a screen, and no study gives a number of elements per diagram, so 15 is a heuristic to be tuned on the case (the MIPT case's second cycle carried 33 elements on three boards, about 11 per board).
- **Errors (record boards):** reported with the schema errors: a record board of an unknown template, with an id of a profile board or template or `records`/`sources`, or twice; an element of a known kind its record board's template does not take. Later: a record board id that is also an element id, a `seen_from` that is neither a position nor a reflexive position, an unknown `unfolds` or one of a kind outside the template's `unfolds`. **Warnings:** a record board with no `seen_from` when its template sets `needs_position`; a kind whose `home` is a template, on another board ("usually lies on a board of template 'process'").
- **Errors (v0.3):** `scheme` on an element that is not a gap or on a thesis that is not a problem; a `detail.keys` column that is not among `columns`; with a taxonomy file, an unknown taxonomy or value in `tags` or in a bound column.
- **Warnings (v0.3):** `tags` or `detail.keys` used and no taxonomy file given.
- **Across cycles:** when the revised record is among the files validated, a new cycle with no new or changed frame, function, model, concept, standard or cooperation gets the hint "reflection without a new scheme". A cycle run by `ingest-transcript` or `theses-to-board` whose new theses feed only elements the previous cycle did not have gets `cells-from-information`: the cells were drawn from the information, not before it (GP-Guide11 ¶5).
- **Robustness rules R1-R6 (`docs/method/robustness-rules.md`):** the errors: a `domain` or `sample` range with min above max; `rivals` naming itself or an unknown hypothesis; `escaped` on an element that is not a signal or note, with no `date`, or naming an unknown hypothesis; `date` with no `escaped`. The warnings: a `sample` name the rule's `domain` lacks; a recorded `confirmed` or `refuted` on a sample outside the domain. The hints: `domain-sample` (a rule with a `domain`, an `observed` with no `sample`; field-result.md §0.2); `surprise` (a hypothesis refuted by its `observed` and by every rival read on the same count: record a dated `signal` with `escaped`; field-result.md §3); `line-without-recovery` now on every profile and for every red line (`docs/red-lines.md`). The verdict hint adds `flips`, the unknown-case outcomes and the part of the domain a sample covers.
