# Method notes: sources

Prepared 2026-10-25. English text, Russian terms in parentheses. Every claim carries a source key from the bibliography in section 0. Marks used:

- **[V]** read in the primary text cited (quoted or paraphrased from the fetched text).
- **[S]** confirmed only in a secondary source (catalogue, encyclopedia, later author).
- **[UNVERIFIED]** I could not find it in a primary text; treat as hypothesis.
- **[ANALYSIS]** my own inference from the cited texts, not a claim of the source.

"Classic" means G.P. Shchedrovitsky (GP) and the Moscow Methodological Circle (ММК) up to 1994. "Later adaptation" means P.G. Shchedrovitsky (PG), S.V. Popov, O.S. Anisimov and others after the classic period, and any program-specific usage.
