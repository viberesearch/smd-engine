# ОДИ, problematization and situational analysis

Part of the method notes compiled 2026-10-25 from external primary sources; source keys and claim marks ([V], [S], [UNVERIFIED], [ANALYSIS]) are defined in `sources.md`.

## 4. ОДИ (организационно-деятельностная игра)

### 4.1 Facts and preconditions

- First game ОДИ-1: Новая Утка, 18–26 August 1979, on programming complex research and developments (КИПР) for a consumer-goods range [V GP1983]. Over 90 games were held 1979–1991 [S, calend.ru biography summary in search results; not checked in a primary text].
- Conditions GP calls "necessary and obligatory for any ОДИ" [V GP1983]:
  1. The customer cannot formulate a technical assignment but expects to be led out of a dead end.
  2. The methodologists qualify the assignment as a problem, not a task, and as poly-professional and poly-disciplinary.
  3. Such assignments can be solved only by new forms of organizing collective МД.
  4. Nobody, including the methodologists, has the means of solving it or of organizing the needed collective work; these must be found in the game. Goal formula: "go there, I don't know where; bring that, I don't know what".
- The program is deliberately built on practically impossible volumes of work; GP made this an obligatory principle, since the main purpose is development of МД and of participants [V GP1983].

### 4.2 Roles and positions

- In ОДИ-1 preparation, four competing foci of organization and control arose: a project-approach co-leader, a group of young methodologists for situational programming and situational control, a research leader demanding a rigid research program, and the overall organizer-leader insisting on a free, searching style [V GP1983].
- PG: GP introduced four positions at the first game: руководитель игры, методолог, игротехник, исследователь. Later the researcher dropped out (its chronotope does not match the game), then the separate methodologist; in games of the second decade only руководитель and игротехник remained [V PG-GT, later author's account].
- Three functions of the игротехник [V PG-GT]: (1) work with the board and its content; (2) work with understanding and communication; (3) work with reflection of individuals and groups, including entry into and exit from the game. PG rejects mapping these onto moderation/mediation/facilitation because it drops the methodological reflection that moves between the three.
- Organizers (methodologists, game technicians) play before the game by simulating it in thought; participants are drawn into the game only to the degree the organizers themselves play [V PG1987].
- Seminar positions "критик", "организатор дискуссии", "арбитр" are described for the ММК seminar, not for ОДИ [S Anisimov, a later author]. "Участник" is used throughout; a canonical list including "критик" for ОДИ: [UNVERIFIED].

### 4.3 Phases of ОДИ-1 (the only fully documented phase list I found)

[V GP1983]

| Phase | Days | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| Preparatory stage | 25 days, 11 four-hour sessions in Moscow | concept, aims, org-project, program, plan, scenarios for key moments, functional structuring of organizers; a "small game" for organizers |
| 1. Introductory | 2 | discussion of the theme; forming group cores |
| 2. Self-determination (самоопределение) | 1 | установочные доклады: concept, aims, program, schedule; methodological scheme of programming; self-determination of participants relative to aims, situation and others |
| 3. De-objectification and problematization (распредмечивание, проблематизация) | 2 | decomposing professional МД into мыследействие, reflection, М-К, understanding, pure thinking; problematizing the game situation and the wider sociocultural situation |
| 4. Buffer | 1 | half rest, half reflexive-theoretical discussion of the theme |
| 5. Construction | 2 | drawing the program, assembling material into tabular forms ("пустографки"), composing modules |
| 6. Results and reflection | 1 | reports; reflexive analysis of the game's course, relations, forms of communication |
| Exit stage | after | post-game reflection (train discussion; four commission sessions), which turned into ОДИ-2 |

Phase names "проблематизация, схематизация …" as a fixed canonical sequence for all games: [UNVERIFIED]. PG gives four "through" processes of ОДИ technology instead of a phase sequence: problematization (проблематизация), schematization of the meaning field (схематизация), org-activity use of new schemes, called self-determination or positioning (самоопределение, позиционирование), and objectivation (объективация) [V PG-GT].

### 4.4 The generic dynamic of a game in terms of the МД scheme

[V GP1987; GP1983]

1. Mixing representatives of different professions creates a "mess" (месиво) of МД fragments; contradictions, ruptures and conflicts follow.
2. These force participants into reflexive positions: a shift along "reflexive verticals". In М-К this appears as situational analysis, goal-setting and situational problematization (ситуационный анализ, целеопределение, ситуативная проблематизация).
3. The organizer introduces blank sheets or marked boards: an extra plane, marked as reflexive or as the plane where the collective's МД is drawn, against the plane of the common object.
4. De-objectification (распредмечивание, "unfreezing"): only what existed before is taken apart.
5. Participants start to understand the cultural and social meaning of opponents' positions. Need for co-organization is felt "by the end of the third day, at the latest by the start of the fourth".
6. Second shift: from М-К into pure М; search for schemes. Once schemes appear, "situational analysis turns into analysis of situations" (as objects, not as frames and conditions of МД).
7. Double work with schemes (object-ontological and org-activity); multi-dimensional space with planes of values, goals, means, procedures, object content.
8. Design and programming of future МД in de-objectified forms (situational, table-typological, structural-functional).
9. Immediate enactment ("trying on") in мД; successful systems are fixed as patterns and normed; failed ones are dropped or reprocessed in the next reflexive cycle.

### 4.5 Rules, plenums, reflection

- An outside observer sees "several working groups, group, general and reflexive sessions, an order of themes"; this is only the surface regulation (регламент) [V PG1987].
- In early games each day ended with a slot of about two hours: "Reflection of the general session, of the group's results for the day, and preparation of the group's plan for the next day" [V PG-GT].
- "There are no rules in ОДИ"; the only rule is to keep all planes of МД (external, played, game-proper) in reflection when building moves. Org-project and program function as rules for prospective reflection, but participants do not perceive them so in the first days [V PG1987].
- Self-determination questions for a participant during the установочный доклад (modal reflection) [V PG1987]: What is fixed in the documents, and what is a zone of search? What is required of groups by the program, and what is the group's own initiative? What must one do in a given position? What must I do? What can I really do given training, abilities and profession?
- GP on reflection that "did not look like reflection": "Functionally it was reflection" [V PG-GT, anecdote].
- Typical errors [V PG1987]: reading the program as a schedule; asking for rules (an attempt to turn the game into a non-game); reproducing the game form without improvisation, which degenerates into a performance; only retrospective reflection, which loses the game's specific sense.

## 5. Problematization, problem situations, situational analysis

### 5.1 Definitions

From GP-PP [V]:

- A goal becomes a task (задача) when a method of reaching it exists; professionals take any goal non-reflectively as a task.
- Problem-free task solving can go on endlessly ("решание головоломок", after Kuhn); "no reflection, no thinking as such, no understanding".
- The shift: "I have not a task but a problem". At first it means only "not a task": not a wrong method but no method at all. The situation becomes a problem situation (проблемная ситуация).
- Questions ("How do you do it? Why so? Could it be done otherwise?") only provoke reflexive exits; "a problem is not a question". "A problem is in essence knowledge about ignorance" (знание о незнании).
- Problematization starts from a change of the object of reflection: from the world of nature and things to the world of one's own activity. Questions about causes of difficulties look like object questions but are questions about one's own МД.
- Difficulties and ruptures (затруднения, разрывы) are what triggers goal formation and reflexive exit; they are not yet problems [V GP-PP; GP1975].

From GP-Guide9 [V], a second criterion: a problem arises not when one is right and another wrong (that is an error), but "when two people say opposite things and both are right"; a paradox (апория, антиномия) shows a weak place in the system of concepts. Then one must stop looking at the object and turn to one's means of analysis, constructing new concepts (Galileo's two velocities; Cantor's cardinality).

### 5.2 Procedure of problematization (GP-PP)

1. Make a reflexive exit with respect to one's own thinking and activity.
2. Take a structural-functional scheme of the activity. For task solving, the scheme of the method has four blocks: product, initial material, means of thinking and activity, method.
3. For each block ask: do I have and know it? Mark plus or minus. Typical conclusions: vague idea of the product, so vague goals; no material; inadequate means and methods.
4. The result is knowledge of what is not known or not possessed: the problem in positive form.
5. Convert problems into goals and theme-assignments (темы-задания). GP warns that this formal conversion is not yet real work: special procedures of thematization and goal formation are needed.
6. Then build new layers of thinking and activity to reach them, including creating means and methods.

Checks [V GP-PP]:

- Without a structural-functional representation of the activity, problematization is impossible: "empty places" exist only in a functional structure (example: in "1 2 . 4 5 6 . 8 9 10" one sees missing 3 and 7 only because of the number series).
- The number of problems depends on the scheme chosen: four blocks give up to four groups. Using the scheme of a scientific subject gives problems of facts, models, ontological pictures, means, methods, problems, tasks, knowledge forms, systematization, use.
- Different participants with different schemes produce different lines of problematization from one rupture situation.

### 5.3 Situational analysis

- In ОДИ, situational analysis is first a form of М-К work in the reflexive phase; when schemes appear it becomes analysis of situations as objects [V GP1987]. A game theme of 1983 (И-24) was "situational analysis and analysis of situations" [V GP1983].
- GP-Guide9 separates "real situational problems" from "generalized problems of system analysis" and asks to move in both and then join them [V].

### 5.4 "Рамка" and "такт"

- "Рамка" appears in the classic texts in ordinary use and in a few semi-technical ones: methodology as the "frame" of all МД (GP quoted in PG2019); situations as "frames and conditions of collective МД" [V GP1987]; "sets a frame for the analysis of thinking" [V GP-SMD]. A defined procedure "задать рамку": [UNVERIFIED]; likely later practice.
- "Такт": found only once, informally ("все такты игры") in PG1987. As a defined unit of a session: [UNVERIFIED]; treat as program-specific adaptation.

### 5.5 Typical errors

- Treating an error as a problem; treating a question as a problem; treating a difficulty as a problem [V GP-PP; GP-Guide9].
- Problematizing without a named functional scheme [V GP-PP].
- Staying in the object world when the source is in one's own means [V GP-PP; GP-Guide9].
