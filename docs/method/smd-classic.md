# SMD classic: thought-activity scheme, activity schemes, object and subject

Part of the method notes compiled 2026-10-25 from external primary sources; source keys and claim marks ([V], [S], [UNVERIFIED], [ANALYSIS]) are defined in `sources.md`.

## 1. The thought-activity scheme (схема мыследеятельности, СМД), 1987

### 1.1 Origin and status

- The scheme was first drawn in September 1980, in the reflexive discussion after the third ОДИ (August 1980). The trigger was a participant (М.Г. Меерович) who drew a separate individual board next to his position on the group's position diagram and refused to move schemes between his board and the group board. This split individual thinking from group thought-communication and made it visible [V GP1987].
- GP names four base schemes of contemporary methodology: multi-plane organization of knowledge, reproduction of activity, the three-layer МД scheme, and the organizational-technical relation [V GP1987].
- Status: "the МД scheme must be treated not as a model of some real system but as a scheme of an ideal entity", from which other schemes are derived by "system focusing and system reduction" (системная фокусировка, системная редукция) [V GP1987, conclusion]. A session runner should therefore use it as a generator of derived schemes, not as a picture of "what is really there".

### 1.2 The three belts (пояса)

GP1987 uses the notation мД, М-К, М. In GP1987 they lie horizontally one above another [V GP1987]:

| Belt | Russian | What lives there | Rules |
|------|---------|------------------|-------|
| мД | пояс социально организованного и культурно закрепляемого коллективно-группового мыследействования | collective action on "real-practical material"; determined by cultural norms, social organization, goals, means/methods, and "objective laws" | heterogeneous, heterochronous polysystems |
| М-К | пояс полифонической и полипарадигматической мысли-коммуникации | verbal texts | "practically not subject to the right/wrong distinction"; lives by polylogue, contradictions, conflicts and problematizations |
| М | пояс чистого мышления | non-verbal schemes, formulas, graphs, tables, maps, diagrams; logical rules, mathematical systems, schemes of ideal objects, ontological schemes and pictures, categories, algorithms | "strict rules of formation and transformation"; Aristotle's "logos" |

М-К is central: it joins the left (speaker) and right (understander) halves of the scheme; М and мД are its two limits [V GP1987].

### 1.3 Positioners (позиционеры)

The simplified (monological) version polarizes participants into one who thinks while speaking (left) and one who understands (right) [V GP1987]:

- мД belt: acting positioners 1.1, 1.2 … (left) and 2.1, 2.2 … (right).
- М-К, left (speaker): 3.1 text expresses aspects of the мД situation fixed in reflection; 3.2 text expresses aspects of М; 3.3 text relates both.
- М-К, right (understander): 4.1 understanding through reflexive exit into the reality of мД; 4.2 through exit into М; 4.3 by comparing and separating М and мД components; 4.4 understanding within the communication reality itself.
- М belt: positioner 5 builds thought on own мД and its fixation in texts; positioner 6 builds thought mainly from understanding others' texts.

### 1.4 How understanding and reflection cross belts

- The belts are joined into one system by understanding (понимание) and by reflection (рефлексия). Reflection penetrates all processes and is drawn as vertical movements and links [V GP1987].
- Reflexive positions are drawn as black positioner symbols labelled with a pair of numbers, e.g. 1–3, 3–5, 6–4: the first number is the reflected process, the second is the process in which the reflection finds its form and place of fixation. Pairs like 1–1, 3–3 are allowed (reflection fixed in the same process it reflects) [V GP1987].
- Each belt has its own reality (действительность). "It is in principle wrong to establish identity relations" between the three realities; they are mapped onto each other by understanding, interpretation and reflection, and only through re-forming one into another. In some cases organized content is transferred unchanged [V GP1987].
- Five irreducible processes: мыследействие, рефлексия, мысль-коммуникация, понимание, чистое мышление; two equal participants; three levels [V GP1987; restated by PG2019].

### 1.5 Degenerate (reduced) forms: checks a runner can encode

Any belt can isolate itself [V GP1987]:

| Isolation | Result named by GP |
|-----------|--------------------|
| М cut off from М-К and мД | production of sign-knowledge forms with no link to situations |
| М-К cut off | "inactive and thoughtless speech, pure play with words" |
| мД cut off | "inert, mechanical reproduction" without means of meaningful change |
| understanding autonomized | typical of educational activity; understanding displaces thinking |
| reflection autonomized | pathology: reflection of reflection of reflection; "рефлексивная возгонка" in methodology, perseveration in psychology |

### 1.6 Procedure: from meaning-cloud to ideal object

GP1987 describes the path: a "meaning cloud" (смысловое облако) of collective work; fragments held by different members through different understandings; reflection and reflexive communication pack them into a scheme using graphemes; graphemes are referred to the paradigmatic system as language meanings or knowledge content. This is schematization of meaning and idealization of content (схематизация смысла, идеализация содержания). When the scheme with its content is given independent existence, an ideal object appears, which can become the core of a scientific subject (научный предмет) [V GP1987].

### 1.7 Typical errors (from the text)

- Treating M-K claims by right/wrong criteria [V GP1987].
- Identifying the realities of different belts (identity relation instead of re-forming) [V GP1987].
- Studying any of the five processes in isolation; GP: "cannot lead to success" [V GP1987].
- Reading the scheme as a model of a real system [V GP1987].

## 2. Activity schemes

### 2.1 Activity act (акт деятельности)

- GP1975 distinguishes units of activity at different levels: "mass activity" (массовая деятельность, e.g. the whole social organism with the mechanism of reproduction), spheres (production, education, science, design), and "acts of activity" for solving particular tasks; plus "normative" representations (what each person must do) [V GP1975].
- The simplest methodical representation is a block "disassembly box" (разборный ящик) used to identify elements in one's own and others' activity (scheme 3). The block list of scheme 3 is in an image I could not read: [UNVERIFIED] the common reading "goal/task, initial material, means, procedures, product".
- The cooperation-oriented act (scheme 9) is described in text [V GP1975]: object part: Пр (product), ИсМ (initial material), д1…дj (actions applied to material), Ор (tools and any external means; knowledge in sign form singled out among means). Subject part: the individual, the "табло" of consciousness, internal means and abilities. Goal (цель) can be treated as an object or a subject element.
- Acts are "molecules" from which cooperative systems are assembled [V GP1975].

### 2.2 Reproduction of activity and translation of culture (воспроизводство деятельности, трансляция культуры)

From GP1966 [V]:

1. In mass activity the main process is reproduction; translation and learning are parts of it.
2. Reproduction is drawn as cycles from state S1 to state S2 of a social structure.
3. Simplest mechanism: elements (tools, products, people, organizations) pass directly from S1 to S2: "translation of elements of the socium".
4. More complex: elements of S1 serve as patterns or standards (образцы, эталоны) for recreating elements of S2. Patterns move parallel to the states. This splits the socium into production and "culture" (культура = means that ensure restoration of production structures).
5. Reproduction proper requires activity: patterns work only if a person can produce new formations by them. Activity is translated through living carriers (the teacher as a "living carrier" of patterns), through products, and through sign and material means passed in the combinations in which they were used.

GP-SMD adds that culture defined "through the scheme of reproduction of activity, as a fragment of the reproduction mechanism" is the definition that works in methodology [V GP-SMD].

### 2.3 Cooperation (кооперация)

From GP1975 [V]:

- Cooperation structures are first the mechanism of reproduction, then independent structures that must themselves be reproduced.
- Any external ("object") element of an act, or the whole act, can pass into another act and so constitute a cooperative link (e.g. product of one as material or means of another). Non-material links also exist.
- Cooperation develops "vertically" (multi-layer) as much as horizontally; vertical structuring is a precondition for horizontal extension.
- "Organizednesses" (организованности) bind packets of activity: product exchange becoming a standing production link; a common ideal object (scientists); a shared system of means. Layers then "flatten" (сплющивание) when second-layer knowledge is laid over the first, and the cycle repeats.

### 2.4 Reflexive exit (рефлексивный выход)

Procedure as stated in GP1975 [V]:

1. An individual performs activity set by goals, means and knowledge; it fails (wrong product, no material, actions impossible).
2. He asks why and what to do.
3. If patterns exist, the answer is a description converted into a prescription to copy.
4. If no pattern exists, the answer is a project or plan of new activity, which can only be built from analysis of past activities and products.
5. To get that description he "must leave his former position of actor and move to a new position, external both to the past activities and to the future projected one". The new position is the reflexive position; its knowledge is reflexive knowledge.

Consequences [V GP1975; GP1974r]:

- Reflexive absorption (рефлексивное поглощение): the reflecting activity treats past activities as material and the future one as a projected object.
- Reflexive exit can be redescribed as reflexive cooperation between two "activity positions" or "places", not only as one individual's act.
- Basic paradox: reflected and reflecting activities are not equal; they have different objects, means and knowledge, so "no mutual understanding and no communication in the proper sense" is possible between them. Adopting the other's position destroys one's own. Understanding while keeping one's position needs special means that join both views [V GP1974r].
- Types: borrowed position (заимствованная позиция, after Lefebvre) vs reflexive lifting (рефлексивный подъём); meaning (pre-subject) reflection vs subject reflection (предметная рефлексия) once the reflecting position has its own means, methods and ontology [V GP1975].
- Two ways of "returning" to an objective picture after reflexive exit: naturalistic and activity-based positions [V GP1975].
- PG2019 summarises GP's anti-psychologistic move: reflection is the effect of a change of means, assigned to different positions, hence "the result of a change of positions" [V PG2019, a later author's summary].

### 2.5 Positional scheme

I did not find one canonical text titled "позиционная схема". What the classic texts provide [V]:

- Positions are places in cooperation, each with its own means, methods and "vision of the object"; the classic cooperation example is "практик", "методист", "теоретик", "историк" [V GP-OP].
- In ОДИ the diagram of position arrangement during a discussion was called "организационно-деятельностная" scheme [V GP1987].
- GP's own positional discipline: on the org-activity board one works in subject-object terms ("what must I do?", self-determination), which is not research [V GP-SMD].
- [ANALYSIS] For encoding: a position = (place in a cooperation structure, means, knowledge, object representation, tasks). A positioner = the carrier who occupies it. A person may occupy several positions in sequence; the shift is a reflexive event.

## 3. Object and subject (объект / предмет), ontology

### 3.1 Definitions in the classic texts

- "Object is what is transformed, or what one acts with. Hence two or more visions of the object" [V GP-OP].
- Subject (предмет) is "one of the attempts to build a form of synthesis, first of two representations of an object, then of n representations". "The scheme of double and paired knowledge is the methodological equivalent of the concept of subject" [V GP-OP].
- A subject is a projection of activity and also a thing (a fixed organizedness in material); its structure depends on the structure of activity [V GP-OP].
- Special sciences "have no concept of the object, only representations of objects" [V GP-OP].
- With differentiation of subjects, objects differentiate: object of transformation, of analysis, of study, of construction, of reference (объект преобразования, анализа, изучения, конструирования, отнесения) [V GP-OP].
- Blocks of a scientific subject: problems; tasks; "experiential facts"; "experimental facts"; the body of knowledge; ontological schemes and pictures; models; means (languages, concepts, categories); methods and methodics. Four of them (ontological schemes, means, methods, problems) historically come from philosophy and methodology, so methodology carries "doubles" of these blocks [V GP1981].

### 3.2 How positions constitute subjects

GP1981 states the principles directly [V]:

- Methodology accounts for "the difference and plurality of the actor's positions in relation to the object"; it works with many representations of one object, including professional ones, and treats their plurality as an objective feature of the situation.
- Representations are "not checked for truth against each other, because they are simply different".
- The ontological representation built from a chosen set of positions is valid only for that set; this shows the historical, not subjective, character of ontological representations.
- Reconstruction goes through activity: first describe the thought-activity of each professional ("деятельная заинтересованность") that made them represent the object so; then assemble the representations through the representation of activity, and only "when the activities they are linked to enter into cooperation".
- The methodological object is a "matryoshka": activity with its object nested inside.

### 3.3 Ontology and ontological work

- Ontological schemes and pictures belong to the М belt [V GP1987].
- An ideal object appears when a scheme with its content is granted independent existence [V GP1987].
- GP-SMD: "what we set in the reality of thinking, we then ontologize, objectivate, and then realize" [V GP-SMD].
- Two boards: a board of ontologies (horizontal) and an org-activity board (vertical). The subject-object category "gives a false ontological scheme" on the ontology board and is legitimate on the org-activity board; earlier philosophy and science "constantly confused the two boards" [V GP-SMD]. In ОДИ this appears as "double work with schemes: object-ontological and org-activity" (объектно-онтологическая и оргдеятельная) [V GP1983; GP1987].
- GP1981 lists "ontological analysis" (онтологический анализ) among the forms of methodological work. A definition of the phrase "онтологическая работа" as a procedure: [UNVERIFIED] — not found in the texts read.
- PG2019: positions keep the same semantic field whose core is "objectivated meaning, object or ontology" [V PG2019, later author].

### 3.4 Check of the team member's cow example

Example: one cow is a different subject for farmer, vet, butcher, cook.

- Fits: four positions, each with its own activity, means and knowledge, produce different representations of the object; methodology does not ask which is true [V GP1981]. In GP-OP terms they differ also in object type: the farmer's and vet's cow is an object of maintenance or treatment, the butcher's an object of transformation into carcass, the cook's material is meat, not the cow [ANALYSIS on V GP-OP].
- Needs correction on three points [ANALYSIS]:
  1. "The cow" as one common object is not given in advance. In GP1981 the common object is built by a methodologist, through the activities, and only when the activities cooperate (here, a farm-to-table chain). Without cooperation there is no reason to assemble one object.
  2. Предмет is not a "point of view" or opinion. It is an organization of means, knowledge, ontology, methods and problems. A cook's предмет is cooking, with its own objects.
  3. The example easily slides into naturalism: the "real cow" as the true object and the four as subjective views. GP calls the natural object itself one organizedness of МД among others [V GP1981].
- Suggested wording for the tool: "four positions, four object representations; one object only as a construction for their cooperation".
