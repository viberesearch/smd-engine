Part of the method notes compiled 2026-10-25 from external primary sources; source keys and claim marks ([V], [S], [UNVERIFIED], [ANALYSIS]) are defined in `sources.md`.

## 6. TRIZ from Altshuller's texts

### 6.1 Contradictions

- Technical contradiction (техническое противоречие, ТП) as written in ARIZ-85В step 1.1: two forms ТП-1 and ТП-2, e.g. "if there are many lightning rods, they protect the antenna but absorb radio waves" vs "if few, no absorption, but no protection" [V ARIZ85V-1].
- Physical contradiction (физическое противоречие, ФП), step 3.3 macro level: the operative zone during the operative time must be in state X to perform one conflicting action, and must not (or must be in anti-X) to perform the other; step 3.4 micro level: in the operative zone there must be particles of a kind, and must not be, to provide the two macro-states [V ARIZ85V-3].

### 6.2 Ideal final result (идеальный конечный результат, ИКР)

- ИКР-1 (step 3.1), verbatim form: "икс-элемент, абсолютно не усложняя систему и не вызывая вредных явлений, устраняет (вредное действие) в течение оперативного времени в пределах оперативной зоны, сохраняя способность инструмента совершать (полезное действие)" [V ARIZ85V-3].
- Step 3.2 strengthens it: no new substances and fields may be introduced; use available resources (ВПР) [V].
- ИКР-2 (step 3.5): the operative zone during the operative time must itself provide the opposite macro- or micro-states [V].
- Rule 11: only solutions that coincide with the ИКР or are close to it are acceptable [V ARIZ85V-5].

### 6.3 ARIZ-85В steps (parts and steps as on altshuller.ru)

[V ARIZ85V]

1. Analysis of the problem: 1.1 mini-problem (system: purpose, parts; ТП-1, ТП-2; "with minimal changes obtain …"); 1.2 conflicting pair: product and tool; 1.3 graphic schemes of ТП-1, ТП-2 (table 1); 1.4 choose the ТП that best serves the main production process; 1.5 intensify the conflict to a limit state; 1.6 model of the problem (pair, intensified conflict, what the X-element must keep and remove); 1.7 try the standards.
2. Analysis of the model: 2.1 operative zone (ОЗ); 2.2 operative time (ОВ); 2.3 substance-field resources (ВПР).
3. ИКР and ФП: 3.1 ИКР-1; 3.2 strengthen; 3.3 ФП macro; 3.4 ФП micro; 3.5 ИКР-2; 3.6 standards.
4. Mobilizing resources: 4.1 modelling with "little people" (ММЧ); 4.2 step back from ИКР; 4.3 mixtures of resource substances; 4.4 void; 4.5 derived substances; 4.6 electric field; 4.7 field plus responsive additive.
5. Information fund: 5.1 standards; 5.2 analogue problems; 5.3 table 2 of ФП resolutions; 5.4 index of physical effects.
6. Change or replace the problem: 6.1 physical answer to technical; 6.2 check whether 1.1 combines several problems; 6.3 choose the other ТП at 1.4; 6.4 reformulate the mini-problem for the supersystem, repeating upward if needed.
7. Analysis of the solution: 7.1 control (can new substances be avoided, self-regulating substances used?); 7.2 preliminary evaluation with control questions; 7.3 novelty by patents; 7.4 sub-problems (inventive, design, calculation, organizational).
8. Use of the answer: 8.1 change of supersystem; 8.2 new applications; 8.3 use for other problems.
9. Analysis of the solving process: 9.1 compare real course with ARIZ; 9.2 compare with the information fund, record new principles.

ARIZ notes ask to write in "simple, non-technical, even childish words", avoiding special terms that increase psychological inertia [V ARIZ85V-3].

Table 2, resolution of physical contradictions [V ARIZ85V-t2]: separation in space; separation in time; system transitions 1а (merge into supersystem), 1б (system to anti-system), 1в (system has property C, its parts anti-C); system transition 2 (to micro level); phase transitions 1–4; physico-chemical transition (appearance and disappearance of substance).

### 6.4 The 40 principles and the matrix

- Typical principles for eliminating technical contradictions, © Альтшуллер 1973, include 1 дробление, 2 вынесение, 3 местное качество, 4 асимметрия, 5 объединение, 6 универсальность, 7 "матрёшка", 9 предварительное напряжение, 10 предварительное исполнение, 11 "заранее подложенная подушка", 13 "наоборот", 15 динамичность, 17 переход в другое измерение, 19 периодическое действие, 21 проскок, 22 "обратить вред в пользу", 23 обратная связь, 25 самообслуживание, 26 копирование, 27 дешёвая недолговечность, 34 отброс и регенерация, 35 изменение физико-химических параметров, 40 композиционные материалы [V ALT1973 list on altshuller.ru]. Names 24 and 38 did not extract; the usual names (посредник, сильные окислители): [UNVERIFIED against the page].
- The contradiction table (таблица применения приёмов) is Приложение 1 of ALT1973, with 39 parameters (01 вес подвижного объекта … 39 производительность) [V ALT1973 parameter list]. The full cell values were not verified.
- ARIZ-85В does not route through the table: its main line is ТП → model → ФП → resources → standards [V ARIZ85V structure].

### 6.5 Laws of technical system development

[V ALT1979] Statics: completeness of parts; "energy conductivity"; coordination of rhythms of parts. Kinematics: increasing ideality; uneven development of parts; transition to supersystem. Dynamics: transition from macro to micro level; increasing su-field (вепольность).

### 6.6 Transfer to organizational problems: what transfers, what is disputed

- Altshuller: "ARIZ is first of all a tool for solving concrete technical problems"; regular use builds "multi-screen" thinking: system, supersystem, subsystems [V ALT1975; ARIZ85V-3].
- [ANALYSIS] Likely to transfer: the ТП pair format; intensification; operative zone and time; resource inventory; ИКР with "no new elements"; separation in space, time and system level; supersystem shift (6.4); post-solution reflection (part 9). Unlikely to transfer as such: physical-effects index, su-field standards, the 39 physical parameters, micro-level transitions.
- Disputed: benefits and difficulties of practical TRIZ use, including organizational and cultural obstacles, are surveyed in ILE2013 [S, abstract only]. Business-TRIZ schools are later adaptations; I did not verify their claims.
- GP held an ОДИ on inventive activity: И-4 "Выявление средств, методов и техники изобретательской деятельности", November 1980 [V GP1983 list]; its content and any link to TRIZ: [UNVERIFIED].
- [ANALYSIS] SMD vs TRIZ on contradictions: GP's problem as a paradox of two correct statements is resolved by changing means and concepts (reflexive turn to the activity side) [V GP-Guide9]; TRIZ resolves a contradiction inside the object by separation and resources. A runner should label which route a session takes.

## 7. Systems approach in the Moscow circle

### 7.1 GP's two concepts of system (GP1974s) [V]

1. Traditional: elements plus links, an ontological picture that packs three procedure groups: decomposition and assembly; measurement and characteristics; immersion of structure into the whole and extraction. Known paradoxes: simple body vs element; material vs immaterial links. Processes get reduced to structure or parameters.
2. New (from design practice): "to consider an object as a complex system means to represent it successively in four categorial planes: processes of one kind, functional structure, organizednesses of material, morphology"; then unfold morphology again in the same planes, recursively, until the needed concrete representation. Advantage: systems can interact at the level of material without losing autonomy of their functional structures.

### 7.2 Categories and system work [V GP1975; GP-Guide9]

- Category pairs: process and material; structure and organizedness; form; mechanism and construction. Process–mechanism unfolding deprives the initial process of objective existence and assigns objectivity to the processes of the mechanism.
- System boundaries are not thing boundaries; they are set by processes and links. A deputy head is the "axis" where two systems (his superior's and his own) are put on one person [V GP-Guide9].

### 7.3 Methodological organization (GP1981) [V]

- Layers: practices; subjects (scientific, engineering, managerial, design); particular methodological developments; general methodology.
- Methodological work is not pure research; it includes critique, schematization, programming, problematization, construction, design, ontological analysis and norming. Its products (constructions, projects, norms, prescriptions) are checked for realizability, not truth.
- GP cites the thesis of Klir and Sadovsky that "general systems theory" is a metatheory whose objects are concepts, languages, methods and problems of other sciences.

### 7.4 Blauberg, Sadovsky, Yudin

- GP co-organized with Sadovsky and Yudin the 1962 interdisciplinary seminar on structural-systems methods under the Academy's Council on Cybernetics [S BRE].
- Works: BY1973; BSY1977 [S catalogues]. GP1987 credits Yudin with separating the "categorial-explanatory" and the "subject" functions of the concept of activity [V GP1987 citing Yudin 1976, 1978]. The content of BSY's system principles: [UNVERIFIED] — texts not accessible.

### 7.5 Western counterpart, only where operational

Checkland's SSM: root definition of a human activity system checked with CATWOE (Customers, Actors, Transformation, Weltanschauung, Owners, Environmental constraints); PQR formula "do P by Q to achieve R"; rich pictures; CATWOE attributed to Smyth and Checkland [S CHK]. [ANALYSIS] Usable as a checklist on an activity act: T maps to material→product; Weltanschauung roughly to position. SSM has no belt distinction and no reflexive-position notation.
